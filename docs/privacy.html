Privacy Policy

Intro Tools LOL – Wild Rift (com.introtools.lol) · Effective date: 25 November 2026

Bang Zul built Intro Tools LOL – Wild Rift ("the App") as a free, ad-supported app. This page explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The App uses third-party services that may collect information automatically:

  • Advertising (Unity Ads, Adsterra): device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and interactions with ads. Used to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Notifications (OneSignal): a push notification identifier and basic device information, so we can send you notifications about new intros. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone settings.
  • Intro statistics (CounterAPI): when an intro page is opened, a preview is played, or an intro is installed, the App increases an anonymous counter for that intro. No personal data or device identifier is attached to these counters.
  • Crash information: if the App crashes, technical details are stored only on your device so you can copy and send them to us yourself.

Game folder access

To replace the Wild Rift intro video, the App needs access to the game's folder on your device, using Android's built-in storage permission, Shizuku, or iAdb, which you choose and grant yourself. The App only reads and writes the intro video file in that folder. It does not read, collect, or upload any other files on your device.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide the App, show ads, send notifications, and understand which intros are popular. Data collected by the services above is processed by those providers under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Unity Ads
  • OneSignal
  • Adsterra
  • CounterAPI (anonymous counters only)
  • GitHub (hosts the intro files)

We do not sell your personal information.

Security

All network connections made by the App use encrypted HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we use commercially acceptable means to protect your information.

Your choices and data deletion

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.
  • You can disable notifications in your device settings.
  • To request deletion of data associated with your device, email introtoolsml@gmail.com. You can also request deletion directly from the providers listed above.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

The App may contain links to third-party websites, including ads and sponsor pages. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
